CropCare AI v4 - Real-World Prediction Update

Why Google/field images were often wrong
----------------------------------------
The supplied model was trained and validated on the provided dataset at 224x224.
Its Django preprocessing already matched the training notebook. The main issue is
domain shift: web/field photos have different backgrounds, lighting, framing and
may contain crops not present in the model's 38 classes.

What changed
------------
1. Optional known-crop selector on New diagnosis. This is strongly recommended
   for Google/field photos and prevents cross-crop predictions.
2. Five-view test-time augmentation: full resize, center crop, horizontal flip,
   vertical flip and autocontrast. Probabilities are averaged for stability.
3. Consistency checks: low confidence, small top-two margin, unstable crop across
   views, or weak support for a selected crop causes an Uncertain result rather
   than an overconfident wrong diagnosis.
4. Batch results explain the reliability checks used.
5. CSV export records the selected crop.

Important limitation
--------------------
This improves inference robustness but cannot create disease knowledge the model
never learned. The model supports only the crops/classes in ml_models/class_names.json.
For professional field accuracy, retrain/fine-tune on a real-world dataset and
local field photographs, then evaluate on a separate real-world test set.

Upgrade
-------
Copy your existing db.sqlite3 and media folder into this project, then run:
    repair_environment.bat
The new migration adds only DiagnosisBatch.crop_hint and does not delete existing data.
