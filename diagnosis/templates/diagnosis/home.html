Simple workflow

From field photo to reviewed guidance

01

Administrator registers farmer

The main administrator signs in and creates a secure username and password for each farmer.

02

Farmer uploads evidence

The farmer selects many photos, complete folders, PDFs, TIFF frames, ZIP batches or DOCX/PPTX images.

03

Receive AI screening

The supplied MobileNetV2 model returns the crop/disease class, confidence and top-three alternatives.

04

Get expert review

An administrator or agriculture expert confirms or corrects the result and publishes guidance.
